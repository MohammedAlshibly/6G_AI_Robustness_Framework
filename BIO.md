# Author Biography

## Dr. Mohammed A. M. Al-Shibly

Dr. Mohammed A. M. Al-Shibly is an Assistant Professor and researcher specializing in wireless communications, computer networks, and artificial intelligence applications in next-generation communication systems. He received his Ph.D. in Communications and Networking from the International Islamic University Malaysia (IIUM) in 2018.

He currently serves as the Director of the Computer Center at the University of Fallujah, Iraq. His research interests include 5G/6G wireless networks, AI-driven network optimization, graph neural networks, reinforcement learning, cloud and edge computing, cybersecurity, and intelligent communication systems.

Dr. Al-Shibly has authored and co-authored numerous scientific publications in the areas of wireless communications, machine learning, and network performance optimization. His current research focuses on improving the robustness, reliability, and real-world deployment of AI-enabled 6G communication networks.

### Research Interests

* 5G and 6G Wireless Networks
* Artificial Intelligence for Communications
* Graph Neural Networks (GNNs)
* Reinforcement Learning
* Network Optimization
* Cloud and Edge Computing
* Cybersecurity
* Internet of Things (IoT)
